ELMERALD

A Home for Artistic Freedom. A Space for Possibilities.

ELMERALD is an independent music label and creative platform founded by Elmelda Nyaboke Oiriga.

Built on the belief that artists deserve creative freedom, ownership and room to grow, ELMERALD exists to support authentic musical expression, develop talent and create opportunities beyond borders.

Our Vision

To build a creative platform where artists can thrive freely, preserve their individuality and reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To discover, develop and support artists through creative freedom, ownership, collaboration and opportunities for sustainable growth.

Our Values

- Artistic Freedom
- Authenticity
- Creative Ownership
- Integrity
- Individuality
- Collaboration
- Growth
- Possibility

Our Founder

Elmelda Nyaboke Oiriga is the founder of ELMERALD and an independent pop artist from Kenya.

Her vision is to build a platform that gives artists the freedom to create, grow and own their creative journeys.
