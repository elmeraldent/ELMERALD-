Who we are

Different voices.
One creative home.

ELMERALD is an independent music label and creative platform built around a simple belief: artists should have the freedom to create, evolve and remain true to themselves.

Founded by Elmelda Nyaboke Oiriga, an independent pop artist from Kenya, ELMERALD grew from the experience of navigating an artistic journey independently and recognising the need for a more supportive creative environment.

We want to create a place where artists are valued not only for their music, but also for who they are, what they represent and the stories they have to tell. A place where creativity is encouraged, identities are respected and growth does not require becoming someone else.

What began as an idea is growing into a vision for an independent music company that aims to discover, develop, support and empower creative talent, starting from Kenya and reaching beyond borders.

“ELMERALD is not built around one individual. It is built around the belief that every artist has something unique to bring into the world.”
Our vision

A music industry with room for everyone.

To build a globally recognised independent music company that creates opportunities for artists, celebrates individuality and contributes to a music industry where creativity, artistic freedom and authenticity are valued.

Our mission

Support the artist. Protect the identity.

To discover, nurture and support artists through a creative environment that encourages artistic freedom, individuality, professional growth, collaboration and responsible music management.

The person behind the vision

Built from an artist’s perspective.

Elmelda Nyaboke Oiriga is the founder of ELMERALD and an independent pop artist known professionally as Elmá.

Her experience navigating the independent music journey inspired her to imagine a different kind of music company: one where artists are supported without being stripped of their individuality.

Through ELMERALD, she hopes to build more than a label. She wants to create room for other artists to discover their potential, express their creativity and build meaningful careers while remaining true to themselves.

Meet our first artist

Elmá

Recording artist · Pop

Elmá is an independent pop artist from Kenya and the first artist under ELMERALD. Driven by emotion, self-expression and authenticity, her music explores human experiences—from confidence and self-discovery to vulnerability and connection.

Her approach is centred on creating music people can relate to, feel and find pieces of themselves in. She continues to explore her sound, develop her identity and connect with listeners beyond geographical boundaries.
